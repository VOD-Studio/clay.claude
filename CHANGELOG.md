## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-11

### Added

- Clay Dark and Clay Light themes, generated from the vendored Clay palette.
- `make preview` to try a theme for one session without changing settings.
- `make check` for theme freshness, key coverage and the design checklist.
- Versioning: `make release` and version checks in `make check`.
